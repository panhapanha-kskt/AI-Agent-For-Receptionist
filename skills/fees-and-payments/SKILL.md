# Fees and payments

> EXAMPLE DATA - replace with your academy's real information.

- Exact fee amounts are in `fees.yaml`. Always read that file before quoting a price; never guess.
- Fees can be paid per term (3 terms) or per year. Paying the full year gives a 5% discount.

## When to hand off
- Payment problems, refunds, or a specific family's balance: take a message for the Finance Office.
